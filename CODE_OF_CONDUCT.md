# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on technical discussions and outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive or intentionally hostile conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributors that violate this policy.

## Reporting

Report concerns through repository maintainers or GitHub reporting tools.
